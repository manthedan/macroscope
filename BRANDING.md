## One-line tagline

```text
Macroscope: developer environment archaeology for your Mac.
```

## Design notes

- The microscope lens is aimed at a tiny `MAC` target instead of using the Apple logo, which keeps the banner plain ASCII and avoids relying on special glyphs.
- The vibe should feel like a precise local diagnostic tool, not a generic cleaner app.
- The phrase **developer environment archaeology** fits the project better than **cleanup**, because the tool explains history and ownership before suggesting action.
