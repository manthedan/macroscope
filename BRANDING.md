## One-line tagline

```text
Macroscope: developer environment archaeology for your Mac.
```

## Design notes

- Avoid large ASCII wordmarks unless they are clearly legible at terminal widths.
- Prefer a small microscope icon or no logo over a noisy banner.
- The phrase **developer environment archaeology** fits the project better than **cleanup**, because the tool explains history and ownership before suggesting action.
